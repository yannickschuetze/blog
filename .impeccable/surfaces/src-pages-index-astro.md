# Surface brief: yannicksblog (whole site)

Mode: Read. Pages: home index, Geschichten list, Projekte list, post page, Über mich, 404, RSS. German only. Astro, Markdown posts, static output. Replaces the earlier "Ulmer Raster" world at the owner's request ("too clean", wanted it "more personal with character" and narrower on desktop).

Audience: friends, colleagues, strangers arriving at one post or wanting to know who writes. Job: find a story or project, read it comfortably, find the next one.

Content: none exists yet. Placeholder posts carry `placeholder: true` and are labelled on screen; never invent biography. Drawings are supplied later by the owner; until then each slot shows a clearly labelled placeholder. The reference image belongs to a real studio: its name, logo and cat drawing are never copied.

States: 0 posts (empty state), a few, 100+ (index grouped by year). Long titles wrap. Posts with and without a drawing or figures.

Out of scope: comments, analytics, dark mode, newsletter, tags, search.

Open: hosting, domain, the owner's drawings.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A sketchbook-poster blog: flat pastel grounds, black ink, condensed capitals and the owner's own drawings as the main character, in one centred 960px column. It refuses both the clean grid it replaces and the cream-and-serif "warm blog".

OWN-WORLD: The page is drenched in one flat pastel: pink (#ffc4c0 range) for home and Geschichten, butter yellow for Projekte; no white cards, no second accent. Everything else is black ink: condensed display capitals with a slightly hand-cut edge for wordmark, nav, titles; a calm sans for running text; 2px black rules; hand-drawn black drawings. Wordmark is a black block with knocked-out capitals.

STORY: The reader sees a person's playful sketchbook, reads the big title, picks a numbered post, reads it in a calm column on the same pastel, and moves on via previous and next.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar: black-block wordmark left, capital nav centred, Über mich right. Then a two-line centred capitals title, a short sans line under it, and a large drawing (placeholder until supplied) centred beneath, its top in the first viewport. Primary action: the index's first post title just below the drawing.

FORM: Pinned whole by the owner: "The blog should be minimalistic and playful like the reference image. you can add placeholders where I will add drawings" (reference: pink ground, black condensed capitals, large hand-drawn black creature). seed: none (pinned). Signature interaction: drawings and titles wobble slightly on hover; read posts get a hand-drawn tick in the index.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
